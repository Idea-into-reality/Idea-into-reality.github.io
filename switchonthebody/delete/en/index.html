SwitchOn Body Data Deletion

App: SwitchOn Body: Diet Planner (kr.ideality.switchonthebody) · Developer: IIRN (Idea-into-reality)

SwitchOn Body has no accounts and never sends what you enter or your body photos to our servers. Everything is stored only on your device, so you can delete it yourself right away.

Delete everything in the app

1. Open the Settings tab. 2. Tap Reset all data at the bottom. 3. Tap Reset in the confirmation dialog. Your data is deleted immediately.

Uninstalling the app also deletes all data on the device. You can also delete body photos one by one on the photo screen.

What is deleted

  • Age, sex, height, weight, activity level and weekday routine
  • Schedule check and skip records, items you added, and times you changed for a day
  • Weight and body composition records (entered by you or imported from Health Connect)
  • RealBody photos and analysis results
  • Scheduled reminders and home screen widget data

We keep no copy after deletion.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Google AdMob processes device information such as the advertising ID to show ads. Google, not us, controls this data. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads on your device, and manage Google data in your Google Account.

Emails you sent us

Emails sent from "Send feedback" are deleted without delay once your inquiry is resolved, and kept for up to 1 year only when needed for a dispute. Contact us below to have them deleted sooner.
